# Google Play organization enrollment preparation

Planning basis: CalorieBank is a Health / Nutrition and Weight Management app;
use organization enrollment, not a personal-account workaround. No account, payment,
terms acceptance or identity attestation is authorized/performed by this document.
Checked current official Google guidance September 18, 2026.

**Founder status:** legal organization existence/name/address and D-U-N-S number are
UNKNOWN. Nothing supplied proves readiness; this is not a claim that no organization
exists. The prior Play session displayed signup, not an enrolled account dashboard.

## Must have before starting a usable enrollment

| Item | What founder must establish |
| --- | --- |
| Actual organization | Legal name and address matching its verified organizational records; no invented CalorieBank legal entity |
| D-U-N-S | Correct nine-digit identifier for that exact entity; search existing records first. Google says acquisition can take up to 30 days. Request is normally free; limited unsupported-region/government alternatives require Google support, not self-exemption |
| Account owner/authority | Intended long-term owner Google account, authorized adult representative/contact identity and authority to accept agreements |
| Organization website | Real organization website and ability to prove ownership; prepare Search Console/DNS/website-owner cooperation as prompted |
| Contact channels | Organization phone, contact name, Google-contact email/phone and public developer email/phone; ensure OTP/call access and continued monitoring. Public developer details are not interchangeable with a private personal contact |
| Evidence and payment readiness | Organization registration evidence and representative government identity documents if requested; supported credit/debit payment method for the US$25 one-time fee (not prepaid) |

Do not send identity documents, card numbers or legal registrations in chat/repo.
Founder supplies those directly to Google's verified enrollment flow when needed.
A proposed domain alone does not establish organization ownership.

## Can complete during Google enrollment

- Choose the verified organization type/details and developer display name (which
  may differ from legal name); provide organization size, phone, website and contact
  details actually requested by the current form.
- Link an existing suitable **organization** Google Payments profile or create one
  in the flow. Its legal name/address must match D-U-N-S; do not reuse a mismatched
  personal profile simply because payments are not planned.
- Verify contact and developer email/phone through OTP/calls. Google states public
  developer phone verification follows identity and organization website verification.
- Complete website ownership and identity/document checks. Founder performs legal
  certifications and agreement acceptance personally; agent can inspect status only.
- Pay registration fee personally after reviewing the exact charge and terms.
  No payment/enrollment action was attempted here.

Timing: budget **up to 30 days for D-U-N-S acquisition**. Google identity, website,
payments and contact reviews are separate and depend on documents and Console status;
no guaranteed end-to-end approval date is established by this audit. Follow the
actual displayed review estimate rather than promising immediate enrollment. App
review and testing release availability are separate from identity verification.

The newer separate Android Developer Console for non-Play distribution is not a
substitute for this Google Play enrollment. Do not confuse its fees/timing/exemptions
with Play Console requirements.
