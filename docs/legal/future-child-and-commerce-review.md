# Internal future child-account and Commerce review boundary

Not public policy copy. Not authorization to implement or launch features.
Current V1 is not designed for children and has no supervised-account system or
DOB/age gate. Future family intent does not make this release child-directed.

Before child/supervised accounts or CB Watch launch, a dedicated milestone must review:

- Google Families requirements and truthful target-age declarations;
- COPPA and applicable regional child/privacy/health laws;
- parental consent, neutral age screening/age assurance and evasion handling;
- Clerk authentication eligibility and each connected provider's age eligibility;
- health-data access, separation, retention and deletion;
- advertising/product-recommendation restrictions, including health-derived targeting;
- parental visibility, permissions, controls, account transitions and child autonomy;
- CB Watch identity, device ownership, data flows and authorization architecture.

Before any future Commerce health-derived advertising/personalization, review privacy
policy, meaningful consent, platform restrictions and child safety **before launch**.
A policy update alone cannot authorize a use prohibited by Health Connect/HealthKit
or other provider policies. Preserve the approved no-sale-of-personal-health-information
commitment. No future feature description appears in public V1 candidates.

## Current Play recommendation — founder approval required, not submitted

[Google target-audience guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en)
requires selecting only ages actually designed for and appropriate to the app;
13–15 and 16–17 can include children under local law. “Not designed for children”
is not automatically a global numeric eligibility rule.

Recommend **18 and over as this release's target-audience declaration only**, subject
to founder/legal approval of suitability and current connected-provider eligibility.
This is not an implemented age restriction, permanent product identity or new minimum
age in the privacy policy. Do not select teen groups solely to avoid an 18+ description.
If founder intends current teen participation, establish suitability/legal/provider
eligibility first and then approve explicit ranges. Do not toggle Restrict Minor Access
or add an age gate without a separate decision. No child-targeting boxes submitted.
