# Delete your CalorieBank account

CalorieBank is operated by Near Future I-X.

## Delete in CalorieBank

> [EVIDENCE] Exact current UI path and DELETE confirmation verified in source. Physical qualification evidence supports completion; no new deletion was performed during this audit. Successful live-record deletion does not establish backup erasure deadlines.

1. Sign in and open **Settings**.
2. Select **Delete Account**.
3. Read the confirmation and type **DELETE**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.

Successful deletion removes your CalorieBank account and its associated live records:
imported health totals and activity summaries, bank history, goals, preferences,
saved provider credentials, and notification registration and delivery records.
It also deletes your CalorieBank sign-in identity in Clerk. If deletion has not
finished, the app explains that you can retry; the service can resume a pending request.

Deleting CalorieBank does not delete your Google/Gmail, Fitbit, FatSecret, Cronometer
or other external account. It does not erase the original data in those services,
Apple Health or Health Connect. Manage Apple Health and Health Connect permissions
separately through your device's health/privacy settings.
