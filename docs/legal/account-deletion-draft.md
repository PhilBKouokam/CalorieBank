## Delete in the app

1. Sign in to CalorieBank and open **Settings**.
2. Select **Delete Account**.
3. Read the confirmation and type **DELETE**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.

Deletion removes your CalorieBank account and associated live account data, including
imported health totals, history, goals, preferences, saved provider credentials and
notification registration/delivery records. The service disables device notifications,
performs required connected Fitbit authorization cleanup, removes the CalorieBank
Clerk identity and deletes the internal account records. If deletion cannot finish,
the app offers a retry and the service can continue a pending request.

This deletes your CalorieBank identity, not your Google/Gmail, Fitbit, FatSecret or
Cronometer account. Original tracker entries and Android Health Connect data are not
erased by CalorieBank account deletion. Health Connect access remains controlled by
Android; manage it separately in Health Connect app permissions.
