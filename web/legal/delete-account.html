Delete your CalorieBank account

CalorieBank is operated by Near Future I-X.

Delete in CalorieBank

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Select Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation and type DELETE.
  4. Tap Delete Account.

Successful deletion removes your CalorieBank account and its associated live records: imported health totals and activity summaries, bank history, goals, preferences, saved provider credentials, and notification registration and delivery records. It also deletes your CalorieBank sign-in identity in Clerk. If deletion has not finished, the app explains that you can retry; the service can resume a pending request.

Deleting CalorieBank does not delete your Google/Gmail, Fitbit, FatSecret, Cronometer or other external account. It does not erase the original data in those services, Apple Health or Health Connect. Manage Apple Health and Health Connect permissions separately through your device's health/privacy settings.

Copies may remain in backups or operational and security logs after live account deletion. Hosting, authentication and notification services have separate retention practices; deletion does not recall notifications already accepted for delivery.

If you cannot access the app

Request deletion from CalorieBank Support at:

support@caloriebank.philbk.dev

If possible, write from the email associated with your CalorieBank account. We need to verify that the request is yours before deleting the account. Do not send your password, sign-in verification codes, food diary or unnecessary health information. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.
